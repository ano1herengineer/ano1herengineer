## 👋 About Me

I’m an **SAP ABAP Developer** focused on building, enhancing, and supporting custom solutions in SAP ERP. My technical work includes **ABAP reports, ALV, DDIC, enhancements, forms, interfaces, and archive-aware data retrieval**.

I care about the engineering behind the solution: understanding existing business logic, making focused changes, preserving established behavior, following coding standards, and troubleshooting issues from the data source through to the final output. I share reusable technical patterns and learning notes without exposing employer- or client-specific implementation details.
